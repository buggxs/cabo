Cabo Board is your digital scorepad for the card game CABO. Forget pen and paper: the app adds up the points of every round, applies the rules automatically and lets you focus on what matters – winning!

SCORING WITHOUT MISTAKES
• Enter each round's points with a fast number pad.
• Automatic score calculation, including penalty points for a failed Cabo call.
• Made a typo? Long-press the last round to correct it.
• See at a glance who deals in the current round.

YOUR RULES
• Total score that ends the game (default: 100).
• Kamikaze rule with adjustable points.
• "Exactly 100": hit the total score exactly and your points are cut in half.
• The round winner scores 0 points (can be turned off).
• Special rounds are marked with icons, so nobody loses track.

PLAY TOGETHER
• Publish your game and share a QR code.
• Your friends join on their own phones and see the scores live.
• You can also join with the game ID.

HISTORY AND STATS
• Resume an unfinished game at any time.
• The game history keeps all your games, rounds and play time.
• Final ranking with detailed stats, best round and win streaks.
• Recent player groups are remembered, so the next game starts in seconds.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Choose between the light, modern design and the classic dark green one.
• Available in English, German, Spanish, French and Polish.

Whether you are new to CABO or a seasoned pro, Cabo Board makes every game easier and more fun. Did you know? "Cabo" means "end" in Spanish – exactly what you call out when you want to win!
